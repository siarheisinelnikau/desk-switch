Desk Switch 0.9.3 shows where devices are connected and where shared keyboard and mouse input is active.

- Each computer card now has an **Attached** row for detected keyboards, mice, headphones or speakers, microphones, and displays. It refreshes when hardware is unplugged or connected to another computer. Hover over an icon to see the detected device names.
- The active card and tray quick controls follow confirmed input handoffs and returns. The **Shared** row shows the current sharing routes separately from the attached hardware.
- Settings show **Unavailable** when a manually selected device disappears from that computer.

Desk Switch 0.9.2 and 0.9.3 can share devices during a rolling update. Downloads are available for Windows x86-64, Ubuntu x86-64, and experimental macOS for Apple Silicon and Intel.
