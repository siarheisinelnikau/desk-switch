Desk Switch 0.9.2 fixes tray clicks on Ubuntu GNOME when the panel has just created or recreated the Desk Switch icon.

- Left-click opens quick controls as soon as the Desk Switch icon is clickable. Right-click opens GNOME's native menu.
- Quick controls are also available from the tray menu. Enable **Desk Switch Tray** once in the GNOME **Extensions** app. After updating the extension, sign out and back in so GNOME loads the new code.

Desk Switch 0.9.1 and 0.9.2 can share devices during a rolling update. Downloads are available for Windows x86-64, Ubuntu x86-64, and experimental macOS for Apple Silicon and Intel.
