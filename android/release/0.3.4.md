Desk Switch Remote 0.3.4 adds workspace pairing and improves input under continuous movement.

- Pair your phone once with the workspace. The desktop workspace directs touchpad and keyboard input to its active computer.
- Keep sending input while cursor previews are being delivered. Motion queues preserve key and button transitions during bursts.
- On Ubuntu, the phone explains when the GNOME companion is unavailable and resumes preview when capture becomes available.

Update the experimental desktop build on the workspace computers to use workspace pairing.
